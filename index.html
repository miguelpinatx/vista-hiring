How hiring works with the hiring office
  1. You request a position. Tell us the job, how many, full or part-time and the shift.
  2. We post the job and screen applicants. We call them and check their availability.
  3. Good fits show up under New. Claim the ones you want so other stores can’t.
  4. You call and set the interview. Save the day and time here so it’s on our calendar. If they ask about pay, let them know the hiring office will go over it.
  5. You decide: hire or pass. If you hire, add the first day you discussed and whether you got their Food Handler and TABC copies.
  6. We send the offer and onboarding, then email you and the new hire the first-day details. Pay is covered by the hiring office in the offer, so please don’t discuss it.
  7. First day: welcome them, tour the store, share store contacts, start the training videos and keep a copy of their FH and TABC.
